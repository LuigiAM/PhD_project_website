Let's Work Together

MEMoPAD is in its final co-design phase, with thesis submission expected by the end of 2026 and the viva by March 2027. I'm now exploring the next steps so that people can actually use this tool, including commercialisation, collaborations with the NHS, and clinical trials. If you could help MEMoPAD get there, I'd love to connect.

For Researchers & Clinical Partners

Help take emotion monitoring from co-design into clinical evaluation

  • Clinical trials and evaluation studies of MEMoPAD in real care pathways
  • NHS collaborations with services supporting people with anxiety disorders
  • Academic collaboration on emotion recognition algorithms and co-design methodology
  • Joint grant applications (NIHR, UKRI, EU Horizon, etc.)
Let's discuss collaboration

For Investors & Grant Bodies

Back the move from research prototype to a tool people can actually use

  • £10,500 in competitive funding secured from NHS, UWE Bristol and industry to date
  • 7 co-design phases with patients, carers and clinicians (up to 25 participants per phase)
  • Working prototypes of the patient app, smartwatch app and clinician dashboard
  • Designed from day one to fit into clinical pathways; exploring commercialisation routes
Explore funding opportunities

For Product & R&D Teams

Bring co-designed emotion monitoring into wearable health products

  • Real-world use of emotion monitoring on consumer wearables (Google Pixel Watch 2)
  • Licensing, partnership and commercialisation conversations
  • Expertise bridging clinical research, user-centred design and technical implementation
  • Deep understanding of clinical and user needs for mental health features
