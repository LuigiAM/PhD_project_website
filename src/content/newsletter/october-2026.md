The most demanding stage of the whole project is behind us. Ten people living with anxiety disorders took the MEMoPAD prototype into their daily lives for up to five weeks. They then told us in online focus groups what worked, what didn't and what they would change. Thank you: your honesty and enthusiasm are what make co-design so precious! 💚

## 🩺 A last call to clinicians

We're not finished yet. Now we want to hear from mental health clinicians: are the insights the MEMoPAD prototype produced useful in practice, as we discussed in earlier co-design phases? I'll show you some examples (pseudonymised charts and summaries) and ask whether they could bring real value to your practice.

- A 15-minute online interview
- From now until the end of November
- A £15 voucher or charity donation as a thank-you

Already signed up through our clinician survey? Book a time. New to MEMoPAD, and you work with clients with anxiety disorders? Fill in the short sign-up survey first.
👉 [Clinician sign-up survey](https://go.uwe.ac.uk/codesignClinicians)

Know a clinician who might be interested? Please forward them this email.

## 🏆 UKRI Mental Health Platform Research Summit: a surprise in Sheffield

On 8 October I gave my short talk at siesta time, very much focused on my digestion, and I didn't even know there was a prize. So I was quite surprised to hear my name announced as winner of the Dermot O'Reilly Memorial Prize, for the most innovative research and engaging presentation by an early career researcher.

Thank you to MQ Mental Health Research, DATAMIND UK and the UKRI Mental Health Platform for an amazing event.

## What's next

After the clinician interviews, the final survey I mentioned last month will go to everyone who signed up through the recruitment form. It's now coming a little later, by the end of the year. Our co-design methodology deserves to be analysed and evaluated, as this might provide useful insights to future research.

📅 On Wednesday 9 December, 12:00–13:30, I'll give a 15-minute talk on MEMoPAD at a UWE Bristol research seminar on "Technology Enhanced Care in Long Term Conditions", including first lessons from this final phase. Glenside campus. Thanks Fiona Cramp for inviting me to present!
